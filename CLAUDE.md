# Chairlift website (usechairlift.com)

Static site for Chairlift, hosted on Netlify. Every push to `main` publishes the live site.

## Working with the owner
- The owner (Ryan) is not a developer. Explain things in plain English, keep answers short, and give step-by-step instructions when he needs to click something.
- **Only push to `main` when Ryan says "publish".** Each publish uses Netlify credits (Personal plan, ~15 credits per publish). Batch changes; save work-in-progress on a `preview/...` branch.
- Never invent clients, testimonials, statistics or results. Illustrative examples must not be called case studies.
- Never use the old name "The Marketing Consulting Company".

## How the site is built
- Pages are generated by `tools/build.py` (content) and `tools/common.py` (shared head, header, footer, contact form). Edit those, then run `python3 tools/build.py`. Don't hand-edit the generated HTML.
- Shared styles: `styles.css`. Shared behaviour (phone menu, contact form): `script.js`.
- Contact form posts to FormSubmit, which emails ryan@usechairlift.com.
- Email signatures are generated by `tools/signature.py` into `brand/`.
- Old URLs (/services, /about, /contact) redirect via `_redirects`.

## Brand guidelines: keep them current
- The brand guide lives at `brand/index.html` (usechairlift.com/brand/, noindex, not linked from the site).
- **Whenever a brand element is added or changed** (logo, colours, fonts, accent rules, layout rules, voice, signature, assets), update `brand/index.html` and add a dated line to its change log in the same change.
- If Ryan says "teal" or "mint" he means Chairlift Neon.
- Colour names: **Midnight Ink** `#141B2A` ("Ink") and **Chairlift Neon** `#3DFFC1` ("Neon"). Neon is an accent only: one key word per title, the hero arrow, link underlines, small labels on Ink. Never on title bars, never as text on white.
- Layout: straight section edges, no staircase graphics outside the logo, white cards on Ink sections, two Ink sections never touch.
- Font: Hanken Grotesk (headings ExtraBold 800, ALL CAPS). Email: Helvetica/Arial.
- Voice: professional and friendly. Don't assume a prospect's size, budget or competitors.
