How it works.

Three stops to the top.

Three steps, built around how your team already works. Your people are part of it the whole way.

Base lodgeMountain hotel
Stop 1

Onboard

We learn how your team actually works today: where the hours go and what frustrates people most.

What happens

  • A conversation with you about how the business runs
  • Short chats with the people closest to the work, where it helps
  • Mapping the process as it really happens, not as it's written down

What you come away with

  • A shortlist of what's worth fixing first, in order
  • A clear recommendation for where to start

What we'll ask you

  • What does your team do every week that eats the most time?
  • What happens after a quote goes out?
  • What do people keep asking each other for?
  • What happens to leads that come in after hours?
  • If you could get rid of one repetitive task tomorrow, what would it be?
Stop 2

Ride

We fix one task inside the tools you already use, and refine it with the people who use it.

What happens

  • Building on your existing tools, with access you grant
  • Testing with real examples before anything goes live
  • Adjusting wording, timing and hand-offs with your team

What you come away with

  • One working fix your team actually uses
  • A plain-English explanation of what it does and why
Stop 3

Summit

Your team gets time back for the work that matters. Then we look at what's next, together.

What happens

  • Checking it's running the way it should
  • Gathering feedback from the people using it
  • Deciding together whether there's a next process worth tackling

What you come away with

  • A team with fewer repetitive tasks on their plate
  • A clear next step, or a natural place to stop

Common questions.

Can't see yours here? Ask us directly.

Is this a chatbot, or outsourced?

No. You work with our own team of real people, never outsourced. If a chatbot is the right fix for one of your tasks, we can build one, but you'll always be dealing with a person.

What happens in the five minutes? Is it a sales pitch?

We ask what slows your team down and tell you honestly whether there's a practical fix. If there isn't, we'll say so.

Is this going to be expensive?

We won't quote until we understand the job, and you'll see the price before any work starts.

I don't have time for another project.

It starts with five minutes, and after that we do the building. Your team just shows us how the work gets done today.

We tried software before and nobody used it.

That's why we don't sell software. We fix one task inside the tools you already use, and refine it with the people who use it.

Can I trust you with my accounts?

We're our own team of real people, never outsourced. We only touch what the fix needs, and you stay the owner of your tools and data.

We're probably too small for this.

If your team does the same task every week, you're not too small. We start with one task and only go further if it's worth it.

I don't want robots talking to my customers.

Neither do we. You always deal with real people, and nothing goes to your customers until you've signed off on it.

Do we need to buy new software?

In most cases, no. We build inside the tools you already use: your inbox, calendar, spreadsheets, and the booking or quoting software you already pay for.

If something genuinely needs adding, we'll explain why before anything changes.

Do we need someone technical on our team?

No. You need someone who knows how the work gets done today. We handle the technical side and explain everything in plain English.

Is this about replacing staff?

No. It's about taking the repetitive parts of the job off people's plates, so they can spend more time with clients and on the work they're good at.

How long does it take?

We'd rather do one thing well than ten things halfway. Once we understand how your business runs, we'll give you a realistic timeline for your first fix.

What happens if something stops working?

If something isn't working the way it should, you know exactly who to tell.

Ready to take the first step?

We'll tell you if there's a practical way to make it faster, whether or not we end up working together.
