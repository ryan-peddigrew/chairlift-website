Starter offer

Easier mornings, on one page.

A one-page morning briefing built around how you work: your goals for the day, your schedule and your follow-ups. Get it on paper, by email or right on your desktop, and know what matters before your first coffee.

What's on the page.

  • Five goals for the day, must-dos first.
  • Your schedule for the day, at a glance.
  • Follow-ups: what's on you, and what you're waiting on from others.
  • A few extras you choose, like the weather or a thought for the day.

No printer? It comes by email or right on your desktop. However you get it, it's one page, with no scrolling.
