## Two ways to use a trade show
1. **Exhibit** (book a booth): we want planners, venues, agencies or festival organizers to find us.
2. **Walk / sponsor** (no booth): the show itself is full of buyers of booth AV (exhibitors, exhibit builders, the show's official contractor). Cheaper, and often better for AV.

## How to pick (score each show 1 to 5)
| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---|

Electrical, internet, rigging (if hanging anything), material handling / drayage, furniture, carpet, cleaning, booth staff badges, lead retrieval. At Toronto venues check the official general contractor (for example GES at the Metro Toronto Convention Centre) and union labour rules for who can set up AV.
