## Two ways to use a trade show
1. **Exhibit** (book a booth): we want planners, venues, agencies or festival organizers to find us.
2. **Walk / sponsor** (no booth): the show itself is full of buyers of booth AV (exhibitors, exhibit builders, the show's official contractor). Cheaper, and often better for AV.
