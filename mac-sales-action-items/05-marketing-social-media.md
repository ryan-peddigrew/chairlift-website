## B. Tech photo and video policy (draft for Charles to approve)
**Purpose:** get real, usable photos and clips from every show, safely and with permission.

1. **Ask first.** Before the show, the PM checks the client's media rules (some events are confidential; some venues ban photos). If the event is marked "no media", no photos.
2. **Never during critical moments.** Safety and the show come first. No photos while rigging, operating or when it distracts from the job.
3. **What to shoot:** our gear and set-ups (before doors, empty room), wide shots of stages and lighting looks, load-in / behind-the-scenes, crew at work (with their OK).
4. **What not to shoot:** guests' faces without consent, minors, screens showing confidential content, client logos unless approved, anything unsafe.
5. **How:** landscape and portrait, steady, good light; 5 to 10 photos and one 15 to 30 second clip per show is enough.
6. **Where it goes:** upload within 48 hours to the shared folder `Marketing / Show Photos / [YYYY-MM-DD] [Event]` (no personal social accounts first). Add one line: event, venue, what we supplied, any restrictions.
7. **Use:** marketing reviews before anything is posted; client approval for named posts.
8. **Incentive (from Sean's 15 Sep collateral drive):** monthly recognition or a small reward for the best shot used. [CONFIRM what Charles approves]
9. **Personal accounts:** techs may share approved company posts; do not post client events on personal accounts without approval.
