## Weekly routine (20 minutes, posting owner)
1. Monday: pick the photo from `Marketing / Show Photos`, write or adapt the draft.
2. Monday: send to Ryan / Charles for approval.
3. Tuesday or Wednesday: post on the company page; ask reps to re-share with one line.
4. Friday: note likes, comments, reposts and any inbound message in the tracker below.
