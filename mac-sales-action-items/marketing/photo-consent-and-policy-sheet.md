# Tech photo policy: one-page sheet for crews + photo consent form

Print the policy for the shop wall and the PM binder. Use the consent form for any close-up of a person. Draft: needs Charles's approval before it is used.

## MAC Show photo and video policy (crew sheet)

**Why:** real photos of our shows win work. Stock photos don't. Help us capture them safely.

**Before the show**
- The PM tells you if the event is **No media**. If it is, no photos at all.
- Some venues and clients ban photos. Their rules win.

**During the show**
- Safety and the show come first. Never shoot while rigging, operating or on a lift.
- Best times: after set-up before doors (empty room, lights on), during load-in, and wide shots during the show from the back.

**Shoot this**
- Our stages, rigs, LED walls and lighting looks
- Load-in and crew at work (with their OK)
- Wide room shots where guests aren't identifiable

**Don't shoot this**
- Guests' faces up close without consent, and never children
- Screens with confidential content, client logos (unless approved)
- Anything unsafe or embarrassing for the client

**How**
- 5 to 10 photos and one 15 to 30 second clip per show is plenty
- Landscape and portrait, steady, good light

**Where it goes**
- Upload within 48 hours to `Marketing / Show Photos / YYYY-MM-DD Event name`
- Add one line: event, venue, what we supplied, any restrictions
- Don't post client events on personal accounts. Share the company post instead.

**Reward:** best shot used each month gets [reward approved by Charles].
