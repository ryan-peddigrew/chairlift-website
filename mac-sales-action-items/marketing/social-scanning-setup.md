# Social scanning: setup guide (free tools)

Copy-paste setup so the weekly routine in `../05-marketing-social-media.md` runs in about 15 minutes.

## 1. Google Alerts (google.com/alerts)
Set each to "As-it-happens" or "Once a day", Canada, English, delivered to a shared sales inbox.

| Alert | Why |
|---|---|
| `"MAC Show Systems"` | Mentions of us |
| `"MAC Show" Toronto` | Shorter mentions |
| `Toronto festival 2027 "call for vendors" OR "RFP" OR "request for proposals"` | Festival buying signals |
| `"BIA" Toronto festival 2027` | BIA street festival announcements |
| `Toronto conference 2027 "audio visual" RFP` | Corporate and association RFPs |
| `"new venue" Toronto event space opening` | New venues to pitch in-house AV |
| `"Metro Toronto Convention Centre" 2027 conference` | Big events coming to town |
| `[competitor name]` (one per competitor) | Competitor news |

## 2. LinkedIn
**Follow:** every Hot Lead company in the Master Prospect List, the main GTA venues and hotels, festival organizers, FEO, OBIAA, Destination Toronto, MPI Toronto, CSAE.

**Saved searches (posts, past week):**
- `"event planner" Toronto 2027`
- `"looking for" (AV OR "audio visual" OR "production company") Toronto`
- `"save the date" conference Toronto 2027`
- `festival Toronto 2027 announce`

**Saved people searches (Sales Navigator not needed):**
- Title: `event manager` OR `events manager` OR `meeting planner`, location: Greater Toronto Area
- Title: `director of catering` OR `director of events`, industry: hospitality, location: Toronto

**Job changes:** when a client contact moves companies, that's two accounts. Congratulate them and log it.

## 3. Tender portals (daily, 10 minutes)
CanadaBuys, Bids & Tenders, Ontario Tenders Portal, OECM / Bonfire. Keywords: `audio visual`, `AV`, `staging`, `stage rental`, `sound system`, `event production`, `lighting rental`.

## 4. Reviews
Google Business Profile: reply to every review within a week. After each good show, the PM or rep sends the client the review link.

## Log it
Anything useful goes into HubSpot: a note on the company, or a new deal if it's a real opportunity. Weekly in the sales meeting: top 3 signals and who owns them.
