Booth concepts

Three ways to turn our own inventory into the booth, so the booth is the demo. Top-down plans, not to exact scale. Pick one per show based on the audience and the floor rules. Every concept uses gear we already own, which keeps build cost to graphics, show services and labour.

Concept A · 10 x 20 ft

The Stage

Best forFestival, BIA and concert audiences (OBIAA, FEO, Canadian Music Week)Wow factorHigh: lights, sound and video running all dayWatch forSound limits on the show floor, height limits, rigging rules

Gear from inventory

  • Truss goalpost with moving heads and wash
  • LED wall with a loop of real shows
  • Two small line arrays (low level) and a demo console
  • Stage deck with skirting
Concept B · 10 x 10 ft

The Control Room

Best forQuiet halls: planner and association shows (CMEExpo, CSAE, MPI)Wow factorMedium: hands-on, conversationalWatch forKeep it uncluttered; one strong video loop

Gear from inventory

  • LED wall or large display as the backdrop
  • Mixing or video switcher surface as the counter
  • Headphone stations to hear a show mix
  • Black drape sides, two LED uplights
Concept C · 10 x 20 ft corner

The Venue Corner

Best forVenues, hotels, galas and weddings (CMEExpo, bridal shows)Wow factorMedium-high: shows the room transformation buyers pay forWatch forCorner space costs more; book early

Gear from inventory

  • Drape wall with colour-changing uplights
  • Screen, projector, lectern and speech mic
  • One dressed table (borrow decor from a vendor partner)
  • Small speaker pair for background music
