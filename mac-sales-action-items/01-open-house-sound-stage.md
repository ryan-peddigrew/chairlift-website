**Owner:** Sales team (suggest one named lead plus one operations partner) · **Target:** mid to end of January 2027
**Status:** Plan drafted. Needs decisions on venue space, date and budget before invites go out.

> Items marked **[CONFIRM]** are assumptions. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.

## What we are doing and why
Build a permanent sound stage / long-term rental space at the shop, then launch it with an open house ("party") where clients and prospects walk through the stage, see the gear working, meet vendors and hear live music. The open house is a sales event: every guest should leave with a follow-up booked.

**Goals (set the numbers as a team):**
- Show the sound stage as a bookable product (rehearsals, shoots, product launches, content capture, long-term rentals). [CONFIRM what it will be rented for]
- Put the full inventory in front of planners, venues and agencies in one afternoon.
- Turn warm prospects from the Master Prospect List (Hot Leads, warm LinkedIn intros) into booked meetings.
- Capture photo and video for the website, LinkedIn and proposals (see the tech photo policy in `05-marketing-social-media.md`).
