# Proposal cover emails (drafts)

Send from your own account. Attach the proposal PDF and the RMS "Quotation - MSS Deal" PDF (quantities and totals only). Strip any internal notes first.

## Sending the proposal
**Subject:** [Event name]: our proposal and quote

Hi [first name],

Thanks again for walking me through [event]. Attached are our proposal and quote #[number].

In short: [one sentence on the scope, e.g. "a full general session build with LED, a hybrid stream and four breakout rooms, run by one team from set-up to tear-down"]. The total is $[amount] plus HST.

Two things I'd like to confirm with you:
1. [Open question, e.g. load-in time at the venue]
2. [Open question]

Could we take 15 minutes on [day] or [day] to go through it together?

[Name] · [phone]

## Follow-up (3 business days, no reply)
**Subject:** Re: [Event name]: our proposal and quote

Hi [first name], checking that the proposal reached you. Happy to adjust the scope if the budget needs to land somewhere specific. What would help you or your committee decide?

## Follow-up (7 business days, no reply)
**Subject:** Holding [date] for you

Hi [first name], we're currently holding our equipment and crew for [date]. If you'd like us to keep the hold, a quick reply is enough. If plans changed, no problem; just let me know so I can release it.

## RFP submission confirmation
**Subject:** [RFP name / number]: submission from MAC Show Systems

Hi [first name], our response to [RFP] is attached, following the order of the requirements in section [x]. Could you confirm receipt and let us know the expected decision date? We'd welcome a site visit or clarification call if the process allows.

## Won: thank you and next steps
**Subject:** Confirmed: [Event name]

Hi [first name], thank you for choosing MAC Show. [PM name] will be your production manager and will reach out by [date] to book the site visit. Here's what happens next: [3 steps].

## Lost: keep the door open
**Subject:** Thanks for considering us

Hi [first name], thanks for letting us know and for considering MAC Show. If you're open to it, I'd value one line on what tipped the decision; it helps us improve. We'd be glad to be your backup or quote your next event.
