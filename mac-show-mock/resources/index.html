02FAQ

Common questions

What do you need from me to give a quote?

The event date, venue, guest count and the type of event. A floor plan, run of show or technical rider makes the quote more accurate.

Do you provide crew, or just rent equipment?

Both. We can deliver equipment only, or supply in-house technicians who set up, run the show and tear down.

Can you work in a venue that has its own in-house AV?

Often, yes. Venue rules differ, so we confirm the venue's policy early and coordinate with their team.

Do you handle outdoor events and festivals?

Yes. We supply outdoor stages, weather-ready systems and festival production, from a single stage to a full site.

Can you send insurance and WSIB documents?

Yes. We can provide a certificate of insurance and a current WSIB clearance certificate for venues that require them.

03Glossary

AV terms, in plain words

FOH
Front of house: the mixing position in the audience where the show's sound is controlled.
Line array
A column of speakers hung together so sound reaches the back of a large room or field evenly.
Backline
The instruments and amplifiers performers use on stage, such as drum kits, keyboards and guitar amps.
Rider
A performer's list of technical and hospitality requirements.
Stage plot
A drawing showing where performers, instruments and monitors go on stage.
Truss
The metal framework lights, speakers and screens hang from.
Uplighting
Lights placed on the floor to wash walls or drape with colour.
LED wall
A video screen built from panels, bright enough for daylight and sized to fit the room.
Load-in / load-out
The time to bring equipment into the venue and set it up, and to strike it and take it out.
Hybrid event
An event with a live audience and remote viewers or presenters at the same time.
