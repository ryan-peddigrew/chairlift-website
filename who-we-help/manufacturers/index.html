What it looks like in practice.

An illustrative example, not a client case study, so you can picture how it would work for you.

Example · Custom manufacturer

The incomplete quote request

A customer emails asking for a price on a custom part. The drawing is attached, but the material and quantity are missing. The email sits until someone has time to reply and ask.

Before

  1. The request lands in a shared inbox.
  2. Someone eventually replies asking for the missing details.
  3. The customer answers a few days later.
  4. The quote goes out a week after the first email.

After

  1. The customer gets a reply within minutes asking for exactly what's missing.
  2. Once the details are in, the request goes to the right estimator.
  3. The quote is followed up on schedule until the customer replies.
  4. Everyone can see where each request stands.
Typically built with: Your shared inbox, your quoting spreadsheet or order system, and email.
