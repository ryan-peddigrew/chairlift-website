Example · Professional services firm

The late-night inquiry

Someone fills out the contact form at 11:30 pm. They're stressed, and they've messaged two other firms too. Whoever responds first, and responds well, usually gets the call.

Before

  1. The message waits in the inbox overnight.
  2. The first reply goes out mid-morning.
  3. Booking a consultation takes a few more emails.
  4. By then, they've already spoken to someone else.

After

  1. They get a calm, professional reply within minutes.
  2. A short intake form collects the key details.
  3. A link lets them book a consultation right away.
  4. Your team starts the day with the full picture.
