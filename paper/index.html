The Daily Peddigrew

One page for your mornings.

A one-page morning briefing with your focus, calendar and follow-ups. On paper, by email or on your desktop.

Start a conversation

What's on the page.

  • Today's focus: your must-dos, with the details that matter.
  • Your calendar for the day, at a glance.
  • The emails that actually need you.
  • Follow-ups that are still open, with their deadlines.
  • The weather, headlines and last night's scores.

No printer? It comes by email or right on your desktop. However you get it, it's one page, with no scrolling.

How it starts.

Small, quick and built around you.

Step 1

Talk

A five-minute conversation about how your mornings run and what you need to see first.

Step 2

Build

We put together your first page from that conversation, using the calendar and lists you already keep.

Step 3

Refine

You try it, tell us what's missing, and we adjust it until it's genuinely useful.

Where it came from.

The problem. Mornings started with anxiety, then 30 to 40 minutes checking source after source, and still feeling there was more to catch up on.

The fix. One page that pulls the day's priorities, calendar and follow-ups together, sorted by what's most time-sensitive.

The result. A two-minute read. Nothing important slips, and the day starts with a clear head.

For you, or your whole team.

Start with your own morning. If it helps, we can build a page for each person on your team, so everyone starts the day knowing their priorities.

It's also a simple way to see how we work: find the routine that eats your time, and make it simpler. If there's more worth fixing, we can talk about that next.

Talk it through in five minutes.

Tell us how your mornings run, and we'll show you what your page could look like.
