Privacy policy.

Plain English, short, and only about what we actually do. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Double chairTower 5

What we collect

Only what you choose to share with us: what you type into our contact form, the emails you send us, and the booking details you enter when you schedule a conversation through Cal.com.

Visit statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly tool, to count visits and see which pages help. It sets no cookies, doesn't follow you around the web, and doesn't collect personal information.

How we use it

Only to respond to you and to schedule conversations. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for marketing.

Who helps us run this site

Netlify hosts this website and passes form messages to our inbox. Cal.com handles bookings. Google Fonts supplies some of the lettering. Umami counts visits. Each one gets only what it needs to do that job.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to reply to you or to work together. Ask us and we'll delete it sooner.

How we protect it

This site only loads over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), blocks content from unapproved sources, and filters spam out of every form.

Client information

When we work with a client's business information, we use only what the client approves, and only for that project.

Your choices

We follow Canada's federal privacy law (PIPEDA). To see the information we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email ryan@usechairlift.com.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Using this site is also covered by our terms of use.
