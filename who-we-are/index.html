Who we are.

Why we started Chairlift.

Great teams shouldn't lose their best hours to follow-ups and admin work.

Mountain hotelFirst tracks

The gap we kept seeing.

Throughout a career in sales, we've seen up close how established businesses really run. The talent was there. The tools were there.

What was missing was someone to connect the two, so the routine work runs in the background and people can focus on clients, quality and growth.

Chairlift exists to close that gap. We learn how a business actually runs, then put the right fix in place, one at a time, so your team gets the benefit now, not after a big tech project.

What you can expect from us.

Clear communication

02

One thing at a time

We get one process working properly before suggesting the next. Steady beats overwhelming.

03

Built around your people

Every fix should make your team's day easier, so we build it with the people who'll use it.

A note from the founder

“The best teams I've worked alongside didn't need more people. They needed their time back.”

Throughout a career in sales, I've seen the same story in business after business: the follow-ups that slip, the handovers that stall, the hours lost between systems.

Chairlift fixes that, one process at a time, with our own team and no outsourcing. Send a few lines about how your business runs, and you'll get specific ideas back.
