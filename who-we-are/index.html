Who we are

Why we started Chairlift.

Great teams shouldn't lose their best hours to follow-ups and admin.

The gap we kept seeing.

Years in sales gave us a front-row seat to how growing companies really run. The talent was there. The tools were there. What was missing was someone to connect the two, so the routine work runs in the background and people can focus on clients, quality and growth. That's what Chairlift does.

Chairlift exists to close that gap. We learn how a business actually runs, then put the right automations in place, one at a time, so your team gets the benefit now, not after a big software project.

What you can expect from us.

01

Clear communication

Straightforward explanations at every step, so you always know what's being built and why.

02

One thing at a time

We get one process working properly before suggesting the next. Steady beats overwhelming.

03

Built around your people

Automations should make your team's day easier, so we build them with the people who'll use them.

A note from the founder

“The best teams I've worked alongside didn't need more people. They needed their time back.”

A career in sales is a front-row seat to how companies really run: the follow-ups that slip, the handovers that stall, the hours lost between systems. Chairlift exists to fix that, one process at a time. Send a few lines about how your business runs, and you'll get specific ideas back.
