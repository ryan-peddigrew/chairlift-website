01 — About

One group. Every business connected.

MAC Group of Companies is the parent company behind a growing portfolio of specialist businesses.

Each business keeps its own name, team & clients. Behind them, the group is where everything lives & runs: the systems, the processes and the standards that let each one focus on the work it does best.

03 — Approach

How the group works.

One foundation under every business, so each one runs better than it could on its own.

  1. 01

    One parent company

    Every business is owned & operated by the group, with clear ownership and long-term thinking.

  2. 02

    Shared systems

    Every business runs on the same back-office systems, so nothing lives in silos.

  3. 03

    Automated & efficient

    Processes are built once at group level and used by every business, saving time & cutting errors.

  4. 04

    One standard

    The same expectations for quality, safety & service, whichever business a client works with.
